# give me a summary of all the needed pages in the website

Here’s a concise list of all pages you’ll need, based on the proposal and payment addition.

***

## Public pages

1. **Home**

- Hero with main value prop and CTAs (Browse Courses, Register).
- Popular tracks section (iPlace/iRestore, Full Arch, etc.).
- Featured courses, testimonials, latest case studies, and footer.

2. **Courses listing**

- All courses with filters (category, date, price, open seats).
- Course cards with key info and link to details.

3. **Course detail**

- Full course info: objectives, agenda, requirements, instructor, venue, FAQs.
- “Register now” button → payment/checkout flow.
- Sticky registration summary and related courses.

4. **Case Studies (blog) listing**

- List of all case studies with category chips, search, and featured article.

5. **Case Study detail**

- Full article layout with images, headings, quotes, related case studies, and “browse related courses” CTA.

6. **About**

- Academy story, mission, instructors, credentials/affiliations, and CTA to courses.

7. **Contact**

- Contact form, map/location, phone/WhatsApp, email, working hours, small FAQ.

8. **Auth pages (if you give students accounts later)**

- Login, optionally Register/Reset password (can be added in a second phase).

9. **Legal pages**

- Privacy Policy.
- Terms \& Conditions / Refund policy (important for paid courses).

***

## Payment / registration flow pages

10. **Registration form (can be part of Course detail or separate)**

- Collects student info (name, email, phone, university/year, notes).
- Either:
    - Inline form on Course detail, or
    - Dedicated page `/courses/[id]/register`.

11. **Checkout / Payment**

- Page that shows order summary (course, date, price, total).
- Redirect to Stripe/Paymob or embedded checkout.

12. **Payment success**

- “Thank you” page with registration summary, next steps, and email confirmation note.

13. **Payment failed / cancelled**

- Explains that payment didn’t go through and allows retry or contact support.

***

## Admin pages (dashboard)

14. **Admin login**

- Email/password sign‑in for staff.

15. **Admin dashboard home**

- KPIs (total registrations, upcoming courses, revenue), latest registrations.

16. **Courses management**

- List of courses, create/edit/delete course, set capacity, dates, price, status.

17. **Registrations management**

- Table of registrations with filters, payment status, export CSV, quick actions.

18. **Case Studies management**

- List of posts, create/edit post, upload images, publish/unpublish.

19. **Site settings**

- Basic settings (academy name, contact info, social links, email notification settings).

That set covers everything in your proposal plus a proper payment flow and basic admin operations, while staying lean enough for your timeline and budget.
